FAQ's

Common Questions

Do you arrange Dubai holidays from Glasgow?+
Yes. We can plan Dubai holidays from Glasgow, Edinburgh and other airports, including beach stays, city breaks and combined trips with Abu Dhabi.
What is the best time to visit Dubai?+
The cooler months are the most comfortable for sightseeing and the beach, while summer is very hot. Tell us your dates and we'll suggest what suits.
Do I need a visa for Dubai?+
It depends on your passport. UK government advice says British citizens can normally get a free visa on arrival in the UAE, but other nationalities may differ. We check for you — see our Dubai visa help.
Can you arrange stopovers or add Abu Dhabi?+
Yes. We can plan multi-stop trips, Abu Dhabi extensions and stopovers on the way to other destinations.
