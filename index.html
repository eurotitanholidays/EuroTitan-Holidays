FAQ

Questions people ask
before they apply.

What countries do you cover?+
All 29 Schengen member countries including Spain, France, Italy, Greece, Portugal, Germany, Netherlands, Switzerland, Austria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, Sweden, Norway and more. Tell us your destination and we'll confirm all requirements.
Can you help if my visa was refused before?+
Yes — this is one of the most common reasons people come to us. We review what went wrong previously and rebuild the application to address it directly. Our team handles refusal cases every week.
How does pricing work?+
Every applicant gets one all-inclusive service — application, documents, appointment booking, hotel, flights and insurance. Message us with your destination and travel dates and we'll confirm your personalised quote before anything is booked. Embassy visa fees are separate and paid directly to the visa application centre by you.
How long does the whole process take?+
Most applications are submission-ready within a week of receiving your documents. Appointment availability at the visa centre can affect the overall timeline, which varies by country and time of year.
Is Euro Titan Holidays affiliated with any embassy?+
No — we are an independent UK-registered travel and visa consultancy. We are not an embassy, consulate, or government authority. All final visa decisions are made solely by the relevant consulate or embassy.
