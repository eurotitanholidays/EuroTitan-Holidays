FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Planning a European holiday or need Schengen visa advice? Browse our most common questions — or speak to one of our friendly travel experts who will be happy to help.

What destinations do you offer tours to?+
We cover all 29 Schengen member countries across Europe — including Spain, France, Italy, Greece, Portugal, Germany, the Netherlands, Czech Republic, Croatia, Austria, Switzerland, and many more. If you have a specific destination in mind, just get in touch and we'll find the right package for you.
Can I customize my travel itinerary?+
Absolutely. Every itinerary we build is tailored to your dates, budget, and interests — from city breaks to multi-country tours. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll shape it around you.
How do I book a tour with your agency?+
Simply fill in the form on this page, message us on WhatsApp, or give us a call. We'll confirm your requirements, send a personalised quote, and take care of the booking from there.
What is your cancellation policy?+
Cancellation terms depend on your specific booking (flights, hotels, and visa services each carry different provider policies). We'll always explain the exact terms clearly before you confirm anything.
Do you offer group discounts?+
Yes — get in touch with your group size and travel dates and we'll put together a tailored group rate for flights, hotels, and visa processing.
